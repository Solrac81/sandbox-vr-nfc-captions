**Sandbox VR · The Londoner Macau**  
Print this page. Keep a copy at the counter / lobby / photo spot / exit.

Guest link: https://solrac81.github.io/sandbox-vr-nfc-captions/

繁中員工版：[STAFF-zh-Hant.md](./STAFF-zh-Hant.md)
Guest hold-up cards (A5)：[print/](./print/) — EN · 繁中 · 简中

**Floor reality:** Guests **cannot use phones during the VR session** (hands-on). They must hear about the QR caption + free soda **before** play. Staff help with photos on the **guest’s phone** when phones are allowed, then after the session help them open the QR page, copy the caption, post, and claim soda — less friction, more posts.

---

## 1. Goal

Get more genuine guest posts on Instagram / 小红书 with almost no guest friction: brief early → help with photos → after play, assist the caption + post on **their** phone (with permission) → thank them with **one free soda** when they show the live post. Soft sell only. Never block exit or the next session.

---

## 2. Timeline (Before → During → After)

| When | What staff do | Phones? |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Before play** | Brief the group: free soda + we’ll help with photos + caption/post after. Agree a quick photo moment (lobby / photo spot / before or after gear-up). | Allowed — take 3–5 shots on the **guest’s** phone |
| **During play** | Run the session as usual. **No** phone use, **no** QR pitch mid-game. | Not allowed (hands-on VR) |
| **After play** | With permission, assist on the guest’s phone: scan QR → caption flow → Copy → open IG/小红书 → paste → attach photos already on the phone → post → show you → free soda. | Allowed again |

Keep the next session on time. If a group is large, one staff can finish the post assist while another resets the room.

## 3. Before-play briefing script

Say this **before** they put gear on / go hands-on — so they know the offer exists when phones come back.

**English**  
> “Quick heads-up: phones stay away during the game. After you play, we’ve got a ready caption — scan our QR, copy, and post to Instagram or 小红书 with your photos. Show us the live post and we’ll get you a free soda. We’ll also help take a few photos on **your** phone before or after gear-up if you like — just ask!”

**繁體中文**  
> 「提提大家：玩嘅時候唔可以用電話。玩完之後掃我哋個 QR，一按複製貼文，連埋你啲相貼去 Instagram 或小紅書。出示你剛剛發嘅帖，我哋送你一杯汽水。想影相嘅話，我哋可以幫你用**你部電話**喺大堂／影相位／著裝備前後影幾張——同我哋講就得！」

**简体中文**  
> 「提个醒：游玩时不能用手机。玩完后扫我们的二维码，一键复制文案，连同你的照片发到 Instagram 或小红书。出示刚发的帖子，我们送你一杯汽水。想拍照的话，我们可以帮你用**你的手机**在大堂／拍照点／穿装备前后拍几张——跟我们说一声就好！」

Keep it one breath. Smile. Point where the QR will be after. No pressure if they shrug.

## 4. Photo help

**Where (phones allowed):** lobby, photo spot, before gear-up, or right after gear-down — **not** during the VR session.

**How**

1. Ask first: “Want a couple of shots on your phone before we start / after we finish?”
2. Use the **guest’s phone** as the camera. Do **not** use a staff phone as the primary (harder to get photos back to them for posting).
3. Take about **3–5** clear shots (group + a couple of fun angles). Leave the photos in their Camera/相册.
4. Consent: only people who want to be in frame; skip anyone who says no.
5. Hand the phone straight back unlocked only if **they** unlocked it. Never ask for passcode / Face ID / pattern.
6. Remind them: “We’ll use these after the game for the caption post.”

If they already have good photos, skip shooting — go straight to the after-play assist.

---

## 5. After-play assisted flow

Do this **on the guest’s phone**, with clear permission (“Can I help you post real quick?”). Stand beside them; they can hold the phone while you guide, or they can hand it over unlocked.

**Numbered steps**

1. Open Camera → **scan the QR** (or open the guest link). Prefer Safari / Chrome over in-app browsers.
2. Pick **language** (EN / 繁中 / 简中).
3. Pick the **game** they just played.
4. Tap what they **liked** most.
5. Tap how they **found** us.
6. Tap **Copy** on the caption.
7. Open **Instagram** or **小红书** (guest’s choice).
8. New post / story → **paste** the caption.
9. **Attach** the photos already on the phone (the ones from §4 or their own).
10. **Post**.
11. Guest **shows** you the live published story/post.
12. Give **one free soda** per the rules below. Thank them. Move on — don’t delay the next session.

If Copy fails: long-press the caption → Copy, or open the page in the system browser. If the wrong game/language appears: go back / refresh and pick again.

---

## 6. Soda rules

**Default (Carlos can change anytime):**

- **One free soda per guest** who posts (not one per whole group unless Carlos says otherwise).
- Guest must **show the live published** story or post on their phone.
- Same visit only — post and redeem on the **same visit**.
- Soft sell only. **Never block exit** if they decline or don’t post.
- Same guest, same day, same post → **one soda**. Don’t double-redeem.
- Prefer posts that show Sandbox / The Londoner / location when easy — but don’t reject a clear, genuine post just for missing location.
- Staff judgment: if it looks fake or recycled from another venue, politely skip the soda and call a supervisor if needed.
- This promo is **soda only** unless Carlos says otherwise.

---

## 7. Do / Don’t

**Do**

- Brief **before** play so the soda + caption offer isn’t a surprise after.
- Help with photos on the **guest’s** phone when phones are allowed.
- Ask before touching their phone; assist the full post flow after play.
- Use their language if you can (EN / 繁中 / 简中).
- Keep the next session on schedule — assist quickly or hand off to a free teammate.
- Celebrate the post: “Awesome — here’s your soda!”

**Don’t**

- Pitch QR / soda **during** the VR session or while headsets are on.
- Pressure, guilt, or block the exit.
- Unlock phones, ask for passwords / Face ID / patterns, or use staff phones as the main camera.
- Promise discounts / free games — soda only unless Carlos says otherwise.
- Argue about caption wording — the page handles that.
- Hold up the next group while finishing a long post assist.

---

## 8. Roles if short-staffed

**One person can cover the whole loop:**

1. **Brief** the group before play (script in §3).  
2. **Photo help** in lobby / photo spot / before or after gear-up (§4).  
3. Run the session.  
4. **After assist** on the guest’s phone (§5) → soda (§6).

If two people: one resets the room / starts the next briefing; the other finishes photo + post assist at the counter / photo area. QR should stay visible at **counter**, **exit**, and **photo area** (print from `qr.html` / `qr.png` / `qr.svg`).

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| **Page won’t load** | Phone Wi‑Fi / data. Open in Safari / Chrome (not WeChat in-app if it breaks). Re-scan QR or open the guest URL. |
| **Copy fails** | Long-press caption → Copy, or Generate again → Copy. Open in system browser if clipboard is blocked. |
| **Wrong language / game** | Switch language at top, or refresh and pick again. |
| **No photos on phone** | Skip attach or take 2–3 quick shots at the photo spot, then post. |
| **QR damaged** | Fresh print from `qr.html`. |

Still stuck? Note phone type + what they saw → Carlos / shift lead.
