# Staff playbook — QR captions + free soda

**Sandbox VR · The Londoner Macau**  
Print this page. Keep a copy at the counter / exit / photo area.

Guest link: https://solrac81.github.io/sandbox-vr-nfc-captions/  
QR print page: https://solrac81.github.io/sandbox-vr-nfc-captions/qr.html

---

## 1. Goal

Help guests post a fun caption about their Sandbox VR session on Instagram or 小红书 — and thank them with a **free soda** when they show you the post. Soft sell only: never block exit if they say no.

---

## 2. Timing

**Best moment:** right after the session — while photos are still fresh, or while they wait for photos.

**Not during** the safety briefing or while headsets are on.

---

## 3. Script cards

Keep it short. Smile. Point at the QR.

**English**  
> “Had fun? Scan this QR — pick your language and game, copy a ready caption, paste to IG or 小红书. Show us the post and we’ll get you a free soda!”

**繁體中文**  
> 「玩得開心嗎？掃這個 QR，選語言同遊戲，一按複製貼文，貼去 IG 或小紅書。出示你嘅帖文，我哋送你一杯汽水！」

**简体中文**  
> 「玩得开心吗？扫这个二维码，选语言和游戏，一键复制文案，贴到 IG 或小红书。出示你的帖子，我们送你一杯汽水！」

If they look rushed: “No pressure — the QR is also at the exit / photo area.”

---

## 4. Steps for staff

1. After the session (or while waiting for photos), mention the QR + free soda in one sentence.
2. Point to the nearest QR (counter, exit, or photo area).
3. Let them do the flow themselves: **language → game → liked → found us → Copy caption → paste to IG / 小红书**.
4. If they ask for help, stand beside them — don’t take their phone unless they hand it to you.
5. When they show a **published** story/post, give **one free soda** per the rules below.
6. Thank them and wish them a good day. Don’t upsell if they decline.

---

## 5. Soda incentive rules

**Default (Carlos can change anytime):**

- **One free soda per guest** who posts (not one per whole group unless Carlos says otherwise).
- Guest must **show the published story or post** on their phone to staff.
- Prefer posts that show **Sandbox / The Londoner / location** if possible — but don’t reject a clear, genuine post just because location is missing.
- Soft sell only. **Never block exit** if they decline or don’t post.
- Same guest, same day, same post → **one soda**. Don’t double-redeem.
- Staff judgment: if something looks fake or recycled from another venue, politely skip the soda and call a supervisor if needed.

---

## 6. Do / Don’t

**Do**
- Offer after the session, while energy is high.
- Use their language if you can (EN / 繁中 / 简中).
- Point at the QR; let them scan.
- Celebrate when they post — quick “awesome, here’s your soda!”

**Don’t**
- Pitch during briefing or mid-game.
- Pressure, guilt, or block the exit.
- Take phones without asking.
- Promise discounts / free games — this promo is **soda only** unless Carlos says otherwise.
- Argue about language or caption wording — the page handles that.

## 8. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| **Page won’t load** | Check phone Wi‑Fi / data. Ask them to open the link in Safari / Chrome (not WeChat in-app browser if it breaks). Try the printed QR again or type/open the guest URL. |
| **Copy fails** | Ask them to long-press the caption text → Copy, or tap **Generate again** then **Copy**. Some in-app browsers block clipboard — open in the system browser. |
| **Wrong language** | Top of page: switch to English / 繁體中文 / 简体中文, then continue. Or refresh and pick again. |
| **Wrong game in caption** | Go back / refresh, pick the correct game, generate again. |
| **QR looks damaged** | Replace with a fresh print from `qr.html`. |

Still stuck? Note the phone type + what they saw, tell Carlos / shift lead.
